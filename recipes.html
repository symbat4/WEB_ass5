How To Make Ramen?

🌶 Spicy Buldak Ramen

A spicy Korean ramen with rich chicken flavor. Adjust spice level with sauce.

Ingredients

  • Buldak noodles
  • Egg
  • Green onions
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles.
  2. Add spicy sauce.
  3. Add toppings.
  4. Cook for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Serve hot.

🧀 Creamy Cheese Ramen

Creamy ramen with smooth cheese flavor.

Ingredients

  • Cheese noodles
  • Milk
  • Cheese
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles.
  2. Add milk and cheese.
  3. Mix until creamy.
  4. Add toppings.
  5. Serve warm.

🍲 Classic Shin Ramen

Traditional Korean ramen with spicy broth.

Ingredients

  • Shin noodles
  • Broth
  • Egg
  • Mushrooms

Steps

  1. Boil the broth.
  2. Add noodles.
  3. Add seasoning.
  4. Add vegetables.
  5. Enjoy ramen.

Cooking Tips 🍜

Add an egg and cheese to make your ramen even more delicious!
