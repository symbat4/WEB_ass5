Explore Ramen Types

Every ramen style has its own character. Some are spicy and bold, while others are creamy or rich with broth.

01

Spicy Ramen

Spicy Ramen is perfect for people who enjoy strong flavors and a lot of heat. It usually has a rich spicy sauce and a powerful taste.

What makes it special?

  • Hot and bold flavor
  • Rich spicy sauce
  • Strong seasoning
  • Best for spicy lovers

Spice level: 🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️🌶️

Spicy ramen is a great choice for people who love bold Korean flavors. You can add eggs, vegetables, or cheese to customize your bowl.

02

Creamy Ramen

Creamy ramen has a smooth and rich taste. Cheese, milk, or creamy sauces can make the spicy flavor softer and more balanced.

What makes it special?

  • Smooth and creamy texture
  • Rich cheese flavor
  • Milder taste
  • Good for beginners

Spice level:🌶️🌶️

03

Broth Ramen

Broth ramen focuses on a warm and flavorful soup base. It is usually combined with noodles, vegetables, and other toppings.

What makes it special?

  • Deep and savory flavors
  • Warm soup base
  • Vegetable toppings
  • Comforting and filling

Spice level:🌶️🌶️🌶️

Why Korean Ramen?

Korean ramen is popular for its strong flavors, different spice levels, and many ways to customize a bowl. From spicy sauces to rich broths, there is a ramen style for everyone.

How to Choose Your Ramen
